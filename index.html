Zephra: Drift Car Racing Game - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Zephra: Drift Car Racing Game ("the game", "we", "us"), made by Khalil, uses, and why. If you have a question, contact us at devkhalil1337@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your own device: your coins, best times, the car, colour, rims, livery and number plate you chose, the track, laps and difficulty, and your sound settings. This data stays on your device. We do not run servers for the game and we do not receive this data. You do not need an account to play.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which is run by Google. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and how you interact with ads. Google handles this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases inside the game are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Google Play's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. The game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. The Unity runtime may collect limited technical information about your device and the game's performance. See Unity's privacy policy for game players.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the game deletes the progress saved on your device.
  • You can change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy options, and reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • For data held by Google (ads, purchases), use the tools Google provides at myaccount.google.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
